# The Rule of the Isle

**Goal.** Be the first to reach the target score (default **10** victory points). You can only win on your own turn.

## The Isle
19 hexes in a 3-4-5-4-3 shape. Each produces a resource:

| Terrain | Resource | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Witchwood | Blackwood | 4 |
| Ashen Hills | Cinderclay | 3 |
| Gloom Moors | Ghostwool | 4 |
| Blighted Fields | Wraithgrain | 4 |
| Obsidian Peaks | Soul-iron | 3 |
| The Dead Waste | — (starts haunted by the Wraith) | 1 |

Number tokens 2–12 (no 7) sit on every hex but the Waste. Dots show the odds; 6 and 8 (blood-red) are likeliest. Nine harbors line the coast: four **Smugglers' Coves** (3:1 any) and one 2:1 harbor per resource.

## Founding
In turn order each player places an **Outpost** and a **Causeway** touching it; then in reverse order a second pair. The second Outpost immediately yields one card from each adjacent producing hex. Outposts may never be adjacent to another Outpost or Citadel (the *distance rule*).

## A turn
1. **Roll** (an Omen may be played first).
   - Every hex showing the number pays each adjacent Outpost **1** and each Citadel **2** — unless the Wraith stands on it.
   - *Bank shortage:* if the bank can't pay everyone a resource, a single owed player gets what remains; with several owed, nobody gets it.
   - On a **7**: everyone holding more than 7 cards discards half (rounded down); the roller moves the Wraith to another hex and robs a random card from a player with a holding there.
2. **Build & trade** in any order.
3. **End turn.**

## Costs
| Piece | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Causeway | Blackwood + Cinderclay | must connect to your network; 15 in supply |
| Outpost | Blackwood + Cinderclay + Ghostwool + Wraithgrain | 1 VP; must touch your causeway; 5 in supply |
| Citadel | 2 Wraithgrain + 3 Soul-iron | 2 VP; replaces your Outpost (which returns to supply); 4 in supply |
| Omen card | Ghostwool + Wraithgrain + Soul-iron | hidden; not playable the turn it is bought |

## Trade
- **Domestic:** only the active player trades, with anyone, on any terms. Others accept, decline or counter.
- **Maritime:** 4:1 with the bank; 3:1 with a Smugglers' Cove; 2:1 of the matching resource at a specific harbor — you need an Outpost or Citadel on the harbor's coast.

## Omens (25 cards)
| Card | Count | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Warden | 14 | Move the Wraith and rob; counts toward the Greatest Host |
| Relic | 5 | 1 hidden VP, revealed on victory |
| Pathmaker's Decree | 2 | Lay 2 free Causeways |
| Harvest Pact | 2 | Take any 2 resources from the bank |
| Blood Tithe | 2 | Name a resource; everyone else gives you all of it |

At most one non-Relic Omen per turn.

## Claims (2 VP each)
- **Longest Causeway:** an unbroken trail of 5+ of your causeways, strictly longer than anyone else's. An opponent's holding on the trail breaks it. The holder keeps it on ties.
- **Greatest Host:** 3+ Wardens played, strictly more than anyone else. The holder keeps it on ties.
