# Catan Night — How to Play

**Goal.** Be the first to reach the target score (default **10** victory points). You can only win on your own turn.

## The board
19 hexes in a 3-4-5-4-3 shape. Each produces a resource:

| Terrain | Resource | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | Lumber | 4 |
| Hills | Brick | 3 |
| Pasture | Wool | 4 |
| Fields | Grain | 4 |
| Mountains | Ore | 3 |
| Desert | — (the robber starts here) | 1 |

Number tokens 2–12 (no 7) sit on every hex but the Desert. Dots show the odds; 6 and 8 (red) are likeliest. Nine harbors line the coast: four **3:1 harbors** (any resource) and one 2:1 harbor per resource.

## Setup
In turn order each player places an **Settlement** and a **Road** touching it; then in reverse order a second pair. The second Settlement immediately yields one card from each adjacent producing hex. Settlements may never be adjacent to another Settlement or City (the *distance rule*).

## A turn
1. **Roll** (an Development card may be played first).
   - Every hex showing the number pays each adjacent Settlement **1** and each City **2** — unless the robber stands on it.
   - *Bank shortage:* if the bank can't pay everyone a resource, a single owed player gets what remains; with several owed, nobody gets it.
   - On a **7**: everyone building more than 7 cards discards half (rounded down); the roller moves the robber to another hex and robs a random card from a player with a building there.
2. **Build & trade** in any order.
3. **End turn.**

## Costs
| Piece | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Road | Lumber + Brick | must connect to your network; 15 in supply |
| Settlement | Lumber + Brick + Wool + Grain | 1 VP; must touch your road; 5 in supply |
| City | 2 Grain + 3 Ore | 2 VP; replaces your Settlement (which returns to supply); 4 in supply |
| Development card card | Wool + Grain + Ore | hidden; not playable the turn it is bought |

## Trade
- **Domestic:** only the active player trades, with anyone, on any terms. Others accept, decline or counter.
- **Maritime:** 4:1 with the bank; 3:1 at a 3:1 harbor; 2:1 of the matching resource at a specific harbor — you need a Settlement or City on the harbor's coast.

## Development cards (25 cards)
| Card | Count | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Knight | 14 | Move the robber and rob; counts toward the Largest Army |
| Victory Point | 5 | 1 hidden VP, revealed on victory |
| Road Building | 2 | Lay 2 free Roads |
| Year of Plenty | 2 | Take any 2 resources from the bank |
| Monopoly | 2 | Name a resource; everyone else gives you all of it |

At most one non-Victory Point Development card per turn.

## Claims (2 VP each)
- **Longest Road:** an unbroken trail of 5+ of your roads, strictly longer than anyone else's. An opponent's building on the trail breaks it. The holder keeps it on ties.
- **Largest Army:** 3+ Knights played, strictly more than anyone else. The holder keeps it on ties.
